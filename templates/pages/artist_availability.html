How Availability Works
Priority Rules

The system checks your availability in this order:

  1. Date Exceptions (highest priority) - Overrides everything. Use for holidays, tours, or specific date changes.
  2. Weekly Recurring Slots - Your default schedule. Can mark as available or unavailable (blackout).
  3. Double-booking Protection (lowest priority) - Prevents booking you for overlapping shows.
Blackout Always Wins

If you have overlapping Available and Unavailable slots for the same time, the Unavailable (blackout) takes priority.

Example:
Monday: 09:00-17:00 Available
Monday: 12:00-13:00 Unavailable (lunch break)

Result:
✅ Can book 09:00-12:00
✅ Can book 13:00-17:00
❌ Cannot book 12:00-13:00 (blackout)
❌ Cannot book 11:30-12:30 (overlaps blackout)
Date Exceptions Example

On a specific date, exceptions override your weekly schedule:

Scenario:
You're normally available Monday 09:00-17:00
But Sept 2 (a Monday) you're at a festival

Solution: Add a Date Exception for Sept 2, mark it "Unavailable" for the entire day (00:00-23:59)

Result: On Sept 2, you're completely unavailable despite being normally available on Mondays
